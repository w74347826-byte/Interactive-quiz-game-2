About Interactive Quiz Game

A free learning game where you pick a topic, race the clock and climb the leaderboard.

How to play

  1. Choose a difficulty: Easy gives 25 seconds per question, Medium 18 and Hard 10.
  2. Pick one of six categories. Each has 15 questions.
  3. Answer before the timer ends. You can pick only one answer per question.
  4. Check the answer review on the results page, then save your score.

What is included

⏱️Timed questions

A countdown and progress bar keep every round moving.

🏆Leaderboard

Scores are stored by the Python server and filtered by category.

🌙Dark mode

Switch themes at any time. Your choice is remembered.

💾Saved progress

Leave a quiz and resume it later from the homepage.

Make it your own

Questions live in one list at the top of quiz.js. Add a line, or a whole category, and the game shuffles and scores it automatically. Colours are set at the top of style.css.
